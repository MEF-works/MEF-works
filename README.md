## Start here

I’m **Mike**, a systems/product engineer who likes turning unclear operational problems into working software. I work across architecture, debugging, implementation, integrations, deployment, and the operational layer that keeps a system useful after launch.

My strongest areas are **developer infrastructure, AI-assisted systems, platform/integration work, operational tooling, and commerce infrastructure**. I’m especially comfortable entering a messy system, finding the real constraint, and reducing it to something maintainable and testable.

## Open source contributions

I’m deliberately spending more time contributing upstream: reproducing real issues, reading unfamiliar codebases, making scoped fixes, adding tests where useful, and working through maintainer review.

> **External-only ledger:** I’ll add entries here for contributions to repositories I do not control. PRs against my own projects do not count.

**Current focus:** developer tooling, APIs and integrations, deployment/CI, operational edge cases, testing, and architecture-level bugs where a small fix can remove a large amount of friction.
